# 1. Purpose

State, in two to four sentences, why this SOP exists and what it accomplishes.
Name the outcome the procedure guarantees and the risk it controls. Avoid
restating the title — explain the *why*.

> Example: "This SOP defines the standardized process for onboarding a new
> vendor into the procurement system. It ensures every vendor is risk-assessed,
> tax-verified, and approved before the first purchase order is issued, reducing
> compliance exposure and duplicate-vendor errors."
