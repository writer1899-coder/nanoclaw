Read this before you send money to anyone

  • This is a planning estimate, not a filed return. It assumes the standard deduction, one QBI-eligible business, and no other income, credits, or itemized deductions.
  • "Net profit after tax" divides taxes only against this business's gross profit. If you entered other W-2 wages, the federal and NC tax shown are for the whole joint return, so this figure slightly overstates what the business alone "cost" in tax.
  • 2026 federal brackets and QBI/SS figures reflect preliminary post‑OBBBA inflation adjustments reported ahead of the IRS's final published tables — small revisions are possible before filing season.
  • North Carolina starts from federal AGI, not federal taxable income — it does not allow the federal QBI deduction, which this worksheet accounts for.
  • Quarterly figures are your annual estimate divided by four. Real IRS/NCDOR safe-harbor rules (prior-year tax, uneven income via annualization) can change what's actually due on a given date — talk to a CPA if your income is lumpy or this is a high-stakes year.
